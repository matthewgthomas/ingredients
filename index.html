      About the data & sources

      This atlas pairs each ingredient's origin — where its source organism was domesticated, or where a prepared food was first made — with the main regions that grow or produce it today.

      Origins and their coordinates are synthesised from crop- and animal-domestication research and general reference works. Many crops were domesticated gradually and sometimes in more than one place, so origins are approximate and a few are actively debated. Present-day production reflects FAO data and is indicative of the leading producers rather than an exact ranking.

      Each ingredient links to its Wikipedia article — a starting point whose own references trace back to primary sources — and to the relevant FAO production dataset.
